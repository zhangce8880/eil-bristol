We welcome enquiries about doctoral research at the intersection of geospatial science, AI and the environment.

Prospective PhD students

We are interested in hearing from applicants with experience in geospatial science, computer science, data science, statistics or related disciplines.

Start by identifying a research question that connects with the group’s interests. Your enquiry should explain the scientific motivation and how you propose to approach it.

Explore our research themes ↗

What to include

  1. Your CV

    Education and grades, research experience, publications and relevant awards.

  2. A 1–2 page research proposal

    Your question, proposed data and methods, and the connection to our research.

  3. A writing sample

    A paper, dissertation, thesis chapter or other substantial research writing.

Enquiries containing only a CV will normally not receive a response.
