OUR RESEARCH

Geospatial methods.
Environmental questions.

Our research connects methodological advances in AI with questions across physical and human geography.

01

GeoAI, foundation models & multimodal learning

Developing methods that bring together satellite, airborne, street-level and other geospatial observations.

How can models learn across sensors, places and scales? We investigate representations, cross-modal learning and efficient adaptation for environmental applications.

02

Remote sensing & environmental intelligence

Turning Earth observation data into information about landscapes and their change.

Our work connects image analysis and geospatial modelling to environmental monitoring, from individual vegetation features to regional patterns.

03

Environmental change, ecosystems & climate hazards

Understanding ecosystem dynamics, flood hazards and the impacts of environmental change.

We use AI and Earth observation to investigate vegetation, biodiversity and inundation, linking what we observe to the processes and vulnerabilities that matter.

04

Spatial decision intelligence

Connecting geospatial evidence with decisions about socio-ecological systems.

We examine urban resilience and land-use questions, bringing environmental and social information together to support more informed spatial decisions.
